CHAPTER 08 OF 12 · 1969–1972

Ken Thompson & Dennis Ritchie

Unix & C — The Quiet River Underneath
What They Invented

The Unix operating system and the C programming language

In 1969 at Bell Labs, Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie grew tired of giant, slow, tangled operating systems. So they built a small, clean operating system called Unix — built around simple tools that do one job well and snap together with pipes.

To make Unix portable across different kinds of computers without rewriting it from scratch every time, Dennis Ritchie invented the **C programming language**.

C stayed close enough to the hardware to be blazing fast, yet clean enough that humans could read it anywhere in the world.

Why It Still Runs Inside Your Device Today

Linux, macOS, Android, iOS, game consoles, Wi-Fi routers, and even the core of web browsers all run on C and Unix ideas invented half a century ago.

Honest History Note

Simple, honest building blocks outlast giant complicated corporate systems every single time.
