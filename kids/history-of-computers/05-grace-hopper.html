CHAPTER 05 OF 12 · 1952

Grace Hopper

The Compiler — Teaching Machines Human Words
What They Invented

The first compiler (A-0) and machine-independent programming languages (COBOL)

Early programmers had to write raw numbers like 01001101 by hand. Everyone told Rear Admiral Grace Hopper that computers could ONLY do arithmetic and could never translate English-like words into machine numbers.

Grace Hopper proved them all wrong. She built the first compiler — a translator program that lets humans write clear words while the computer automatically turns them into exact machine instructions.

Her team also famously taped an actual moth found stuck inside a Mark II relay into their logbook — making the word "debugging" famous forever!

Why It Still Runs Inside Your Device Today

Every programming language you have ever heard of — TypeScript, Python, Rust, C, Swift — exists because Grace Hopper proved machines can translate human-readable code.

Honest History Note

Grace Hopper’s favorite quote for builders: "The most dangerous phrase in the language is: We’ve always done it this way."
