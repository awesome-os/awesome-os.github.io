1. Dual-Track Adoption Strategy

New operating-system paradigms (B8G-SECT-1, Stealify Composition Algebra, b8g-browser) have a long industry adoption curve. Instead of waiting passively, we run a quarantined Side-Challenge targeting incremental, backward-compatible wins for Google V8 and Chromium:

  • Phase 1 — V8 Build Accelerator (@awesome-os/b8g-cxx): .b8p WASM Trie CAS .obj packs, Shared Transitive PCH, Sub-TU Code-Block incremental compilation (cc-block-chunker), and zero-v8.h C-ABI stubs (v8.ifs / v8.stub.lib).
  • Phase 2 — Mojo / ipcz Zero-Copy SAB Ring Fast-Path: Keep 100% .mojom interface compatibility while accelerating high-frequency local IPC channels underneath Mojo with a lock-free B8G_ARENA_V1 ring.
  • Phase 3 — Blink LayoutNG & Viz Quad Fast-Paths: Bring surgical flat-array layout and 48 B/quad slab fast-paths to Chromium while feeding W3C conformance back into b8g-browser.
